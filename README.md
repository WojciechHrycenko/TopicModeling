# Topic Modeling - Project

## Grading Overview
- **Final grade composition:**
  - **Home-taken project:** 70%
  - **Presentation:** 30%
- **Minimum requirement to pass:** 50% (overall)

---

## Key Dates
| Type | First Take | Second Take |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Presentations** | Jan 15, Jan 22 | Feb 20, Mar 1 |
| **Project Submission** | Feb 8 | Mar 1 |

---

## Project Requirements
- Data **must be independently collected**.
- The **topic should be proposed** after **consultation with the course instructor**.
- The project must include:
  - Application of **at least one topic modeling algorithm**.
  - **Optimization of hyperparameters**.
  - **Concise presentation** of obtained results.
- **Preferred format:** Jupyter Notebook.

---

## Evaluation Criteria (Project)
Projects will be evaluated based on:
1. **Scientific and business relevance** of the chosen topic.  
2. **Clarity** in formulating the project objective.  
3. **Appropriateness** of the selected method for achieving the goal.  
4. **Effort** invested in data cleaning and preprocessing.  
5. **Correctness** of the analysis.  
6. **Quality** of the conclusions.  
7. **Structure and organization** of the report.

---

## Presentation Details
- The presentation aims to **verify independent work** and **understanding of topic modeling fundamentals**.
- Students should expect **questions regarding core concepts**.

### Evaluation Criteria (Presentation)
1. **Correctness** of the content presented.  
2. **Responses** to questions (if asked).  
3. **Structure and organization** of the presentation.  
4. **Adherence to the time limit**.
